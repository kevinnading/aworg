Where the Resident sits

Three ways to put a figure in the chat's top-left. The 28px icon beside replies stays in all of them — this is the presence, not a replacement. Status is drawn from the same live activity the Activities pane reads (label, detail, progress), never asserted, so it goes quiet on its own when nothing is running.

A  ·  The Perch — figure and a thought bubble

The figure stands in the corner and thinks out loud beside itself. The bubble is the status; when there is nothing to say it shrinks to the one quiet line rather than disappearing, so the corner never looks broken.

One thing worth deciding early

The figure above is Nocturne's. Every persona would need its own, which is a real art cost — six personas, six figures, and any persona a stranger writes arrives without one. Cheapest honest fallback: a persona with no figure.svg shows its existing avatar mark at the larger size, and the status bubble works exactly the same. Nothing breaks; it is just less of a character.
