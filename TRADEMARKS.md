# AWORG Trademark Policy

"AWORG" and the AWORG logos are trademarks of Kevin Nading. The license in
`LICENSE` covers the code; this policy covers the name. They are separate on
purpose: you may do a great deal with the code, and still not call what you
made AWORG.

## You may

- Say truthfully that your product is built with, powered by, or works with
  AWORG.
- Distribute unmodified copies of AWORG under its own name.
- Keep AWORG's name and link in AWORG interfaces, as `ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md`
  requires, including in versions you have changed, identified as based on
  AWORG.
- Write about, teach and review AWORG by name.

## You may not

- Name your product, fork, company, service or domain AWORG, or anything
  confusingly similar.
- Distribute a changed version of AWORG as AWORG itself, rather than as based
  on it.
- Suggest that the licensor made, endorses or supports your product when that
  is not so.
- Use an altered AWORG logo, or the logo as part of your own branding.

## Your own product

Anything you build with AWORG gets its own name. You are not required to show
AWORG in it anywhere except in AWORG interfaces.

For anything this policy does not settle, ask before relying on it.
